Who I am

About

I'm a Full Stack Engineer who has built and shipped production systems across every layer of the stack — backend services, user-facing frontends, cloud infrastructure, security tooling, and the architecture that ties it all together at scale.

My career arc has taken me through QA, backend engineering, DevOps, and security — which means I've seen how systems fail at every layer and how to build them so they don't. I bring security thinking into every phase of the product lifecycle, not just as a final audit.

I leverage AI tooling to move faster across the full stack — from rapid prototyping to code review to infrastructure automation. It's a force multiplier, not a replacement for engineering judgment.
